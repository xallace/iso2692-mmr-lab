Why ⌖ Ø Beats ± Coordinates

A legacy ±0.10 mm square tolerance already permits a diagonal corner error of r = 0.10 × √2 = 0.1414 mm. Because bolts and holes are round, any offset within radius 0.1414 mm (Ø0.283 mm) assembles identically! Switching from ± to cylindrical ⌖ Ø unlocks +57.08% more tolerance area for free.

LMR Ⓛ · Protecting Thin Walls

While Ⓜ protects mating assembly clearance, Least Material Requirement Ⓛ protects minimum remaining material — such as wall thickness around a hydraulic port or cast boss. When the hole is drilled smaller than its maximum size (LMS = Ø10.40), extra wall thickness converts into free bonus position tolerance while guaranteeing w_min ≥ (D_min − LMVS) / 2.

When to Use Ⓜ, Ⓛ, or No Modifier (RFS)

Bonus tolerance is one of the strongest cost-reduction tools in ISO GPS — when applied to the right functional features.

USE Ⓜ OR Ⓛ HERE
  • Clearance bolt patterns & locating slots (Ⓜ): Larger clearance holes freely absorb more position error while guaranteeing fastener assembly via fixed Go-Gauge MMVS.
  • Zero-Tolerance-at-MMC (⌖ Ø0 Ⓜ): Gives manufacturing maximum continuous freedom across size + position without arbitrary split limits.
  • Thin-wall bosses & hydraulic ports (Ⓛ): Protects burst strength and minimum wall thickness when holes are drilled smaller than LMS.
DO NOT USE Ⓜ HERE (KEEP RFS)
  • Bearing bores & press-fit dowel holes: Extra radial play as size grows is NOT acceptable — axis position must remain tightly centered regardless of feature size.
  • Gear center distances & optical alignments: Backlash and focus depend strictly on center location, not clearance-hole departure.
  • Non-size features (flat planes, single surfaces): Ⓜ and Ⓛ are legally invalid on features without an opposing size dimension!

Core DIN EN ISO 2692 Equations

Quantity Internal Feature (Hole / Slot) External Feature (Pin / Shaft)
MMS (Maximum Material Size) d_min (smallest hole = most metal) d_max (largest pin = most metal)
LMS (Least Material Size) d_max (largest hole = least metal) d_min (smallest pin = least metal)
MMVS (Go-Gauge Virtual Size) MMS − t_pos MMS + t_pos
LMVS (Least Material Virtual Size) LMS + t_pos LMS − t_pos
Bonus Tolerance under Ⓜ d_act − d_min d_max − d_act
Reciprocity Ⓜ Ⓡ Permits actual size to cross MMS up to MMVS if geometric error stays below t_pos (so total envelope still clears MMVS).
